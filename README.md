Welcome to BIL Study!

BIL Study is a personal learning and productivity platform designed to help students organize their academic activities, improve their productivity, and build better learning habits through a simple, integrated digital workspace.

We believe that learning should be more organized, accessible, and efficient. BIL Study brings essential study tools together in one place, creating a comfortable digital environment for everyday learning.

🚀 Official Launch

We are excited to introduce BIL Study to the public!

This platform is continuously evolving, with a focus on building useful tools, improving the learning experience, and integrating modern technology into everyday academic activities.

✨ Features

BIL Study is designed to bring multiple productivity tools into one workspace.

- Study Dashboard — A central space to access your learning activities.
- Study Timer — Manage study sessions and improve focus.
- Schedule & Calendar — Organize academic schedules and important activities.
- Notes & Drafts — Write, save, and organize study notes.
- Search System — Find your saved information more easily.
- Calculator — Support everyday academic calculations.
- Language Translator — Assist with multilingual learning.
- Progress Tracker — Monitor your learning activities and progress.
- Notifications — Keep track of important activities.
- Data Backup — Support the management and preservation of study data.

Features and availability may evolve as the platform develops.

🎯 Our Vision

To create a connected digital learning environment where students can study, organize, explore, and grow through technology.
